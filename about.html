About Fran

Fran is a thoughtful designer and student developer who cares about accessible digital experiences. She values clarity, calm design, and learning how to build websites that feel welcoming on every device.

Her background blends computer science learning with real design practice. Fran enjoys crafting clean layouts, consistent color systems, and responsive pages that work clearly for everyone.

Fran's design style is simple, polished, and user-friendly. She focuses on clear navigation, strong contrast, readable typography, and a balanced presentation of skills and project work.
